## 3. New Phase 11 / Phase 11.1 Tests Added and Verified

1. `solver::tests::test_cross_check_300_tiny_instances`:
   Exhaustive multi-subject cross-check on 300 instances comparing backtracking solver with brute-force state search.
   - Result: 201 feasible, 99 infeasible, 68 backtracks (33.8% of feasible).
   - Agreement: 100% (0 discrepancies). See `docs/reports/phase-11.1/crosscheck-stats.txt`.
2. `score::bounds::tests::test_lower_bounds_validity_exhaustive_tiny_instances`:
   Tested 200 feasible multi-subject tiny instances with 2 subjects, verifying that exhaustive minimum scores for S1, S6, S8 always equal or exceed the computed lower bounds.
   - Result: 200/200 valid. See `docs/reports/phase-11.1/bounds-validity.txt`.
3. `domain::quota::tests::test_quota_v2_regression_legacy_demo_equals_phase_3`:
   Asserts exact quota v2 parity on legacy demo ($q_t = 36/11$, $\text{lo} = 2$, $\text{hi} = 4$).
4. `domain::forced::tests::test_forced_placement_cascade_propagation`:
   Tests propagation where forcing reviewer role cascades setter role to remaining candidate.
5. `domain::forced::tests::test_forced_placement_infeasible_zero_candidates`:
   Asserts informative infeasible error when zero candidates available.
6. `domain::forced::tests::test_forced_placement_infeasible_excess_pins`:
   Asserts error when PIN locks exceed seat count.
7. `optimize::tests::test_d1_incremental_state_consistency_demo_10k_moves`:
   Verifies incremental vs full score equality and checks `validate_assignments` after all 10,000 accepted moves on legacy demo.
8. `optimize::tests::test_d1_incremental_state_consistency_q_shaped_10k_moves`:
   Verifies incremental vs full score equality and checks `validate_assignments` after all 10,000 accepted moves on Q-shaped dataset.
9. `optimize::tests::test_d1_incremental_state_consistency_synthetic_40_10k_moves`:
   Verifies incremental vs full score equality and checks `validate_assignments` after all 10,000 accepted moves on 40-teacher 2-subject synthetic dataset.
10. `storage::migrations::tests::test_upgrade_from_v1_to_v5_preserves_data`:
    Verifies migration chain from v1 schema directly to v5 schema preserving legacy data.
11. `storage::migrations::tests::test_legacy_equivalence_v4_to_v5`:
    Verifies that a valid v4 schedule upgraded to v5 preserves 36 assignments, has zero hard violations, and produces the exact same score. See `docs/reports/phase-11.1/migration-tests.txt`.
